Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

MeTransfer is a private tool used only by its owner to send files to their clients. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data MeTransfer uses

Google Drive: MeTransfer creates folders and files in the owner's own Google Drive. It shares those transfer folders with a view-only link. It can only access files it created itself.

Gmail: MeTransfer sends one notification email to the recipient from the owner's own Gmail account. It cannot read any email.

Email address: MeTransfer reads the owner's email address only to send them a copy of each notification.

What is stored, and where

Uploaded files are stored only in the owner's Google Drive. A small record of each transfer is kept only on the owner's own computer: the recipient's email address, title, message, dates and status.

Deletion

Each transfer, including all of its files, is permanently deleted from Google Drive 10 days after it is shared. Transfer records are removed after 90 days.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, analytics or AI training.
